## Behaviour references (text only, no media reused)

- 10,000 Birds, "Wiggle like a Woodcock" — description of the rocking walk (feet planted, body rocking 6–8 times before a step, head level)
- Birdshow.blog, "Why Do Woodcocks Walk Like That?" — body sways forward then rocks back with each step, followed by a pause
- Frontiers in Zoology (2014), "On vision in birds: coordination of head-bobbing and gait" — hold/thrust head-bobbing synchronised with the double-support phase; used for the neck-leg coordination
- Sibley Guides, "The mysterious sounds of the American Woodcock" — twittering flight sound from the outer three primaries during the fast shallow wingbeats; used for the falling flutter
